# Data Dictionary

- `stressLevel`: User-reported stress from 1 to 10.
- `heartRate`: Heart rate in beats per minute from manual or wearable input.
- `sleepQuality`: User-reported sleep quality from 1 to 10.
- `breathingIrregularity`: Boolean symptom flag.
- `triggerEvent`: Boolean indicator that an identifiable trigger occurred.
- `riskScore`: Combined 0-100 anxiety escalation risk estimate.
- `confidenceScore`: Confidence estimate for the risk result.
- `escalationProbability`: Estimated probability that intervention/escalation may be needed.
- `voiceStressScore`: Simulated score from acoustic feature metadata.
- `relapseRiskScore`: Estimated 24-72 hour repeat-episode risk.
